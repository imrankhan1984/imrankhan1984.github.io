Profile

I work where data architecture meets business strategy. For 18 years, 11 of them at Schneider Electric, I have designed how data is modeled, governed and delivered, and I have helped leaders agree on what that data should do for the business.

Today I help Schneider's IT and IoT (Internet of Things) data domains raise their data quality and meet the company's data rules. I also lead the case for knowledge graphs across the enterprise, work that is presented to the Executive Committee.

Before that, I designed the data model behind a new electrical product data platform. It cut data processing time from six months to four hours. I have professional experience of working in France and Canada and have led technical teams across the USA, Europe and India. I am at my best in ambiguous situations where technology, people and strategy have to line up, and I explain technical choices in plain terms so decision-makers can act on them.
